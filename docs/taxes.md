# Taxes

## Examples

### LTCG

Let's say I have a position in VTI of $1M at the start of 2027 all held for more than a year. Every month, I sell $10k of VTI for spending. At the end of the year, how much do I owe in taxes (MFJ, Texas) ?

Assuming this is your only household income for 2027, you will owe **$0 in taxes**.

### The Breakdown

**1. No Texas State Income Tax**
Texas does not impose a personal state income tax, so your state tax obligation is **$0**.

**2. Cost Basis vs. Capital Gain**
When you sell $10,000 of VTI per month ($120,000 annually), you are not taxed on the full $120,000—only on the **capital gain** (the growth above what you originally paid for those specific shares). Even if 100% of the $120,000 were taxable gain, your tax would still be $0.

**3. Federal Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) Brackets**
Because you held the shares for over a year, your sales qualify for long-term capital gains tax rates. For Married Filing Jointly (MFJ) in 2027:

* The **0% federal capital gains tax threshold** is projected at **$102,100** of taxable income.
* The **standard deduction** for MFJ in 2027 is projected at **$33,200**.

This creates a tax-free income shelter for long-term capital gains:


$$\text{Taxable Threshold} + \text{Standard Deduction} = \$102,100 + \$33,200 = \mathbf{\$135,300}$$

Since your maximum possible gain from selling $120,000 of VTI is **$120,000** (which is less than $135,300), your taxable long-term capital gains fall entirely within the **0% federal tax bracket**.

---

### Important Nuances to Keep in Mind

* **Qualified Dividends:** VTI currently yields ~1.3%–1.5% in dividends annually. On a $1M portfolio, VTI will generate roughly **$13,000 to $15,000** in qualified dividends over the year. Because qualified dividends are taxed at long-term capital gains rates, adding ~$15k of dividend income to your $120k of gross sales brings total cash/gains near ~$135k, which remains right around the 0% federal tax limit.
* **Net Investment Income Tax (NIIT):** The 3.8% NIIT applies only if your Modified Adjusted Gross Income (MAGI) exceeds **$250,000** for MFJ. You are well below this threshold.
* **Other Income:** If you or your spouse have other sources of income in 2027 (e.g., W-2 wages, 1099 income, traditional IRA withdrawals, or interest), that income stacks *first* and fills up your standard deduction and lower tax brackets, which could push a portion of your VTI capital gains into the 15% bracket.
